When Should You See a Surgeon for Piles?

By Dr. Vamseedhar Konati • General & Laparoscopic Surgeon, Tirupati

Piles, also known as hemorrhoids, are swollen blood vessels in and around the anus and rectum. They are extremely common and often improve with simple lifestyle changes, but some cases need medical or surgical treatment.

Common Symptoms

  • Bleeding during or after bowel movements
  • Itching or irritation around the anus
  • Pain or discomfort, especially while sitting
  • A lump or swelling near the anus
  • A feeling of incomplete bowel emptying

When Home Care Is Usually Enough

Mild cases often improve with a high-fiber diet, adequate hydration, avoiding prolonged straining, and over-the-counter creams — always under medical guidance.

When It's Time to See a Surgeon

  • Bleeding that is persistent, heavy, or recurring
  • Pain that doesn't improve with home care
  • A hemorrhoid that is swollen, very painful, or has prolapsed (come out) and won't go back in
  • Symptoms lasting more than a week despite home treatment
  • Any unexplained change in bowel habits alongside bleeding, which should always be evaluated to rule out other conditions

Treatment Options

Depending on severity, treatment can range from dietary and lifestyle changes to minimally invasive procedures or surgery for more advanced or recurrent cases. Modern techniques aim to minimize pain and speed up recovery.
