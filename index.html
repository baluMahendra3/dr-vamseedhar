About the Doctor

A Trusted Name in Surgical Care in Tirupati

Dr. Vamseedhar Konati is a well-known consultant general and laparoscopic surgeon based in Tirupati, Andhra Pradesh, with over 20 years of dedicated clinical experience. An alumnus of S.V. Medical College and S.V.I.M.S. University in Tirupati, he has successfully performed more than 10,000 surgeries over his career, earning the trust of thousands of patients and families across Tirupati, the wider Chittoor district, and pilgrims visiting Tirumala from across Andhra Pradesh and India.

He currently practices as a Consultant Surgeon at two of Tirupati's most reputed hospitals — Mother Hospital and Helios Multi Super Speciality Hospital — offering advanced, minimally invasive surgical care close to home.

Beyond his own surgical practice, Dr. Konati is passionate about mentorship. He has trained numerous young doctors in laparoscopic surgical techniques, helping build the next generation of skilled surgeons in the field.
