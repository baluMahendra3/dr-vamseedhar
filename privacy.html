This Privacy Policy explains how this website ("we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you visit or use the contact form on this website operated for Dr. Vamseedhar Konati's medical practice in Tirupati, Andhra Pradesh.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect information that you voluntarily provide through the Contact form, which may include:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number
  • Your email address (optional)
  • Any message or details you choose to share

We do not use cookies, tracking pixels, or third-party advertising scripts beyond standard, privacy-respecting website analytics (if enabled) that count visits and popular pages without identifying you personally.

2. How We Use Your Information

Information submitted through the contact form is used solely to respond to your enquiry, schedule appointments, or provide the information you requested. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Third-Party Services

This website may use the following third-party services to operate the contact form and embedded map:

  • Formspree — processes contact form submissions and forwards them by email. See Formspree's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Maps — used to display an embedded map. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Fonts — used to load website fonts.

4. Data Retention

Contact form submissions are retained only as long as necessary to respond to your enquiry and for the clinic's appointment/record-keeping purposes, consistent with applicable medical record retention requirements.

5. Your Rights

You may request to know what information we hold about you, request corrections, or request deletion of your contact form submission by reaching out through the Contact section of this website.

6. Children's Privacy

This website is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent changes.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us via the Contact section of this website or email vamseedharkonati@gmail.com.
